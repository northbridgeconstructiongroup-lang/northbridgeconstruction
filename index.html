About Northbridge

Built for Commercial Construction

Northbridge Construction Group Ltd. is a British Columbia commercial construction subcontractor specializing in interior and exterior wall and ceiling systems.

We work alongside general contractors and wall & ceiling contractors, providing experienced crews, responsive project coordination and dependable execution from start to completion.

Our focus is simple: quality workmanship, reliable manpower and long-term working relationships.
